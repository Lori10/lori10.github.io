Case Study · Innomatik AG · DB mindbox

AI-Supported Objection and Stakeholder Management for Deutsche Bahn

As part of the DB mindbox program, Innomatik AG developed an AI-supported solution to streamline the handling of objections, inquiries, and stakeholder requests associated with large-scale infrastructure and construction projects at Deutsche Bahn. The goal was to reduce the manual effort involved in researching, coordinating, and preparing responses while maintaining reliable and transparent communication.

01 · The Problem

Large infrastructure projects generate a high volume of inquiries, objections, and concerns from different stakeholders, including authorities, associations, interest groups, private individuals, and the media. Project teams need to research relevant information across extensive project documentation, coordinate input from multiple departments, and ensure that responses are complete, accurate, and based on reliable project information.

Manual research and coordination are time-consuming and error-prone. At the same time, teams need a clear overview of which inquiries are still open, which documents require verification, and where feedback from other departments is still missing.

02 · The Solution

Innomatik developed an AI-supported application that assists project teams throughout the response process:

  • Automated document and inquiry analysis: Incoming inquiries are analyzed against relevant project documentation to identify the information required to answer stakeholder requests. This involves advanced OCR processing to extract text and addresses from incoming documents, and LLM-based classification using few-shot prompting.
  • AI-generated response suggestions: The system generates context-aware draft responses based on the available project information.
  • Human-in-the-loop: Project managers review, edit, and refine the AI-generated responses. Final decisions and responsibility remain with the human experts.
  • Central dashboard: A dedicated interface provides an overview of open inquiries, documents requiring verification, and pending feedback from different departments.
  • Self-hosted models and on-premise deployment: The open-source embedding model, LLM, and reranker are hosted locally and served as APIs. The entire project is containerized with Docker and deployed on the customer's servers through a CI/CD pipeline built with TeamCity.

The solution made use of BIT-RAG, a privately developed production-grade Python RAG framework, of which I have been the main contributor. BIT-RAG provides a complete document-to-answer pipeline for enterprise RAG applications and includes specialized processing for complex, unstructured data.

For complex PDF documents, the ingestion pipeline combines techniques such as OCR, document layout detection, and table detection to reliably extract and structure information that would otherwise be difficult to process. The extracted content is then transformed and prepared for RAG through specialized document processing, chunking, retrieval, and ranking methods.

BIT-RAG also supports hybrid dense and sparse retrieval, semantic/contextual chunking, and LLM-based reranking, with integrations for technologies such as Milvus and OpenSearch. This enables the system to retrieve relevant information from large document collections and provide the LLM with the context required to generate grounded responses.

03 · My Contribution

I was responsible for the entire Backend and AI implementation of the Deutsche Bahn project. My contribution covered the complete AI pipeline, including the integration and application of BIT-RAG for document processing and RAG, as well as the backend services required to connect the AI capabilities with the application. I was also responsible for deploying and monitoring the solution on the customer's servers.

In parallel, I was the main contributor to BIT-RAG, developing and extending the framework's capabilities for enterprise document ingestion, unstructured-data processing, retrieval, and RAG-based applications.

04 · Outcome

The solution reduced manual research and administrative effort, accelerated the preparation of stakeholder responses, and made the overall communication workflow more transparent. The combination of advanced document processing, production RAG infrastructure, LLM-based response generation, and human review enabled AI to support the process while keeping final decisions with the responsible project experts.

From a technical perspective, the project combined enterprise RAG, advanced PDF and unstructured-data processing, LLM-based retrieval and generation, backend engineering, and a human-in-the-loop workflow into a production AI application for Deutsche Bahn.
